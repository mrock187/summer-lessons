Daily Flow & Shop Rules

What keeps the system honest.

Daily Flow Rules

Daily Flow is for normal family responsibilities. These jobs do not each earn a Quest sticker — together they earn the day’s Mystery Card.

  • Smooth jobs, no big fight = 1 Mystery Card
  • One reminder is okay
  • One choice prompt is okay
  • Mild complaining is okay
  • Needing help is okay
No card todayNo Mystery Card for repeated refusal, screaming, running away, or turning every job into a negotiation. The job still gets done.

Shop Time Rules

  • Shop opens Tuesday & Friday
  • Count your points together
  • Choose to spend or save
  • Big rewards go on cooldown
  • Parent has final approval
  • Earned points are never taken away
  • Mystery Cards are separate from the shop

Big Sister Intervention Guide

Cards first — the sticker rewards a specific kind action.

Use it in the moment

Big Sister cards are intervention tools first. Reach for one during a William conflict; if no conflict happens, plan one Big Sister Quest later in the day.

1
One card = a sticker
Use a single card in the moment.
2
Two-card combos
On some days, string two cards together.
3
Complete a set
Work all the way through a full Big Sister set.

The sticker is earned for a specific action

  • used words instead of grabbing
  • offered a trade
  • helped William learn something
  • brought him a baby-safe toy
  • asked Dad for help calmly
  • repaired after a conflict
Not earned for merely being near William, or vaguely “being nice.”

Scripts & Quick Reference

What to say when she digs in — plus the edge cases.

The one ruleThe task can shrink, but it never disappears. Three toys instead of ten — but the job still happens.

When she pushes back

She saysYou say
“I don’t want to!”
“You don’t have to want to. The job still happens. Books first, or stuffed animals first?”
“It’s too hard!”
“I’ll get you started. You do the first part, I’ll do the next.”
“Later!” · “In a minute!”
“First the job, then the fun. The timer starts now.”
“No!” (flat refusal)
“That’s okay to feel. The job doesn’t go away — I’ll wait.”
Bargaining for more points
“We don’t make deals for points. This is just what we do.”

Edge cases

Skittle Sprint

A 3–5 min Brain game. Sticker is for the effort. Skittles are for Brain games only — never Shop currency.

Body = free square

Keep Body easy, active, and fun. It gets Peri close to a full set, so she cares about the harder three.

If the day is melting

Do only: one helper job, a 5-min Brain Quest, outside time, evening reset.

“Tiny plan days still count.”
