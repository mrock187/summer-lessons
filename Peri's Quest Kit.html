Parent Daily Card

The whole day in one glance — plus the three rules that make it work.

The day, in order

  • Morning Helper Reset
  • Brain Quest during William’s nap
  • Body / Nature Quest with both kids
  • Evening Reset before dinner
  • Count the points after dinner
  • Daily Bonus if all 4 quests earned

Minimum viable day

On a rough day, keep only: one helper job, one tiny Brain Quest, one body or outside moment, and one calm reset. That still counts.

The three house rules

Do not remove earned points. Repair behavior separately with a logical consequence.
The task can shrink, but it never disappears. Three toys instead of ten — the job still happens.
Award Big Sister points immediately. Catch the kindness the second you see it.

Repair script

“You were mad. It’s okay to be mad. It’s not okay to grab. Now we repair.”

How to teach a chore

For grown-ups
  1. 1Name the job out loud
  2. 2Show the finish line
  3. 3Do one together
  4. 4Set a tiny target — just 5
  5. 5Give the point right away

Mopping = bonus job

For grown-ups

A high-status helper job — not a daily must. Give one small zone, use a timer, let it feel special. It never replaces basic cleanup.

Make the finish line visible

For grown-ups

Point to the bin. Name the shelf. Count the items. Five-year-olds finish what they can see ending.
